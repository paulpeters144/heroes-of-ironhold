# Shield Charge

## Description

Shield Charge is a forward-driving attack that trades the knight's usual
careful footing for a single, decisive burst of force. On cast, the knight
braces the shield low and lunges in the direction they are facing, sliding
clean across the battlefield in a straight line. For the duration of the
charge the knight is a moving hazard: any enemy that the shield sweeps through
is struck, knocked aside, and damaged as it is pushed clear of the path.

The charge has a clear rhythm. It begins with a short wind-up — just enough
time to read the direction on the screen — followed by a fast, committed slide
that covers a generous stretch of ground. The knight stops naturally at the
end of the charge with no lingering commitment, ready to move or attack again
immediately. Every enemy the shield physically passes through takes damage and
is shoved a short distance back along the charge line, so a single well-timed
run can plow through a crowd, and a bad one carries the knight past an enemy
they meant to pin down.

## Purpose

Shield Charge rounds out the knight in three ways the other skills do not
cover.

First, it is mobility. Every other skill asks enemies to come to the knight —
blades fly out, a disc returns to grind, a ward sits and waits. Shield Charge
is the reverse: the knight goes to the enemy. It closes distance in an instant,
lets the knight sweep from one side of the field to the other, and turns the
defensive shield into an offensive tool.

Second, it is a pass-through attack. The blade fan pierces a line but stops
short on contact, and the grinding disc commits to the first enemy it hits.
Shield Charge does not stop for anyone. It damages and shoves every enemy in
its path, which makes it the reliable choice for breaking up a clustered group
of enemies rather than grinding one target down.

Third, it is a positioning weapon. The knockback is not just damage — it is
control. Pushed enemies lose their footing and their attack windows, giving
the knight breathing room, or the charge can be angled to herd enemies into a
corner so the spinning disc or swords finish them off. A good charge sets up
the rest of the kit.

Where the other skills reward attacking inward, Shield Charge rewards reading
the field and committing to a line. It is the knight's direct, honest answer
to a closing enemy: brace, lunge, and clear the way.
